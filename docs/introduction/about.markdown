### <span style="color:#e67e22"> PhD project </span>

During my PhD thesis, I worked under the supervision of [Emmanuel Franck](https://irma.math.unistra.fr/~franck/index.html) and [Yannick Privat](https://yannick-privat.perso.math.cnrs.fr) and closely <span style="color:#800080">collaborate with physicists</span> of the group [Q-Dyno](https://www.ipcms.fr/en/equipe/theoretical-quantum-dynamics-of-nano-objects-dyno/) lead by [Paul-Antoine Hervieux](https://www.ipcms.fr/paul-antoine-hervieux-2/) and [Giovanni Manfredi](https://www.ipcms.fr/en/giovanni-manfredi-2/).

Most notably, I provided quantitative estimates on the minimal time needed to achieve the best approximation of a logic gate on noisy quantum computers and I established strong evidence that the mechanisms at the root of parametric resonance are, in some sense, an optimal way to destabilize a system.
